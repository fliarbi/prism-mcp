6 · Studied vs reported vs patented — four separate lanes, never added

Src: research.geography (global_comparison, claim "air pollution"). Lanes: news = country an article is about; research authorship = the health literature index first-author affiliation; journal country = the health literature index publishing journal's country (not where the study was done); patents = assignee country. The tool reported no window for these lanes (the health literature index covers its full history; patents run to 2024). Its news lane counts India at 14,696, a different match from panel 2's 9,245, so the two are not interchangeable. India is not in the top 12 journal countries, so that bar is absent rather than zero. The largest patent bucket (1,502) has a blank assignee country.

Tool artefact: global_comparison's "focus" block returned India research = 0 and patents = 0, but its own lanes list India with 1,319 first-author papers and IN with 39 patents. The lane rows are used here and the focus block is ignored.

8 · Data quality — trust the map for the right reasons

  • Placement: only 54.9% of matched articles carry a country, 50.2% a state and 47.0% a city (news.geography). Every map shows the placed half, not all coverage.
  • Share vs share: share_pct is a share of placements. Multi-country articles count once per country, so India's 31.2% of placements equals 59.3% of placed articles.
  • Windows: news counts run Jun 2024 – May 2026. Geography runs May 2024 – Apr 2026, bounded by the labelling pipeline. Ingest after June 2026 is 19%, 2% and 0.4% of a normal month (system.freshness), so nothing later is used. Typed research runs 2017–2025.
  • Three India numbers: 9,245 (news.geography, placed), 8,291 (news.trends country=india, Jun 2024 – May 2026) and 14,696 (global_comparison news lane, window unstated). These are different matches, not contradictions.
  • Artefacts: "centre" as a state (most likely the union government); "sirsa" (most likely the minister's surname); "congress", "earth", "winter" and "california" as cities; "punjab" ambiguous between India and Pakistan. Cameroon (671, lift 8.6×) is a high-lift row of the kind the tool's own caveat warns about. A collision with "Centre", which is also a Cameroonian region, is plausible but not verified. Iceland (277) may be the volcanic-eruption coverage.
  • Research series: the typed corpus shows 14,501 papers in 2018 against 4,462 in 2017 and 6,849 in 2019, which looks like an ingest artefact. The title-only series (analysis.history) rises steadily from 2,348 (2017) to 5,158 (2025).
  • Lift is coverage share for this term divided by that place's share of all news. A small place with a few articles can show a huge lift.
