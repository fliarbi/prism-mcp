June 2025 Samsung faced a cascade of legal, competitive and security challenges, from suing a rival over smart‑ring technology to losing a key foundry client and confronting US pressure on domestic production. The company responded by deepening its hardware partnerships, notably announcing a new Snapdragon platform for the Galaxy Z Fold 7 in July. August’s US policy actions—including a 100% tariff on imported semiconductors and talks of an Intel stake—escalated geopolitical risk and threatened Samsung’s supply chain. From September onward Samsung pivoted toward AI, unveiling an AI‑powered smart‑home vision at IFA and launching a joint AI megafactory with Nvidia in October, while also expanding language support and entering the AI eyewear market.
