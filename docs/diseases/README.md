# Disease intelligence

One report per disease with a view for each reader: **policy makers** (burden, attention, geography, trajectory), **doctors** (risk factors with quoted effect sizes, the evidence behind a clinical claim, what disagrees, two interventions head to head) and **scientists** (framing over time, the frontier, the shape of the literature, the gaps). Each report also runs **nine research exploration methods** on its disease and reads each result in depth. Every panel is an MCP tool call.

> Research intelligence, not medical advice: counts, framings and effect sizes are measured from the research and news index and quoted from their sources; they do not replace clinical guidelines or a clinician's judgement.
