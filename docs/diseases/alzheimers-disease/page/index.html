The year in the news

June‑August 2025 saw a wave of novel mechanistic concepts and multi‑target therapeutic candidates, from chlorogenic‑acid nanocarriers and GADD45G regulation to a unifying astrocytic lipidopathy hypothesis that moved focus beyond amyloid‑β and tau. Parallel advances in biomarkers and diagnostics, including the first FDA‑cleared Elecsys pTau181 blood test (Oct 2025) and stepwise primary‑care screening strategies, began to translate mechanistic insights into clinical tools. Clinical setbacks such as Novo Nordisk’s missed primary endpoint (Nov 2025) and the discontinuation of BACE1 inhibitors highlighted the difficulty of achieving disease‑modifying efficacy. The decisive withdrawal of Biogen’s aducanumab in April 2026 marked a turning point, prompting a shift toward anti‑inflammatory approaches, novel delivery platforms, and non‑amyloid targets. The field now emphasizes multimodal interventions—cPLA2 inhibitors, siRNA nanodrugs, temporal‑interference stimulation, and tau‑targeting trials—while expanding affordable blood‑based diagnostics and personalized biomarker signatures.
