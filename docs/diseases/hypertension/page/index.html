Verdict. the first claim has more evidence behind it

Reading. Salt reduction has the larger body of evidence than potassium supplementation, and the tool's verdict says so. Salt has 6,187 papers across both poles against 1,275 for potassium. This comparison measures volume and stance, not the size of each effect; the two interventions still need comparing on effect size in comparable populations.
