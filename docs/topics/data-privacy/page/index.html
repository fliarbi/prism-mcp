The arc

June 2025 opened a wave of breaches across health, municipal and electoral systems, exposing how vulnerable traditional institutions are to privacy violations. July and August saw the crisis deepen as high‑profile lawsuits, a massive Qantas hack and supply‑chain attacks on banks and insurers highlighted both legal and technical fault lines, while researchers warned that AI tools like ChatGPT could be poisoned to leak data. By September and October the focus shifted toward protecting vulnerable groups—children’s data and AI‑driven personalization—prompting executive orders, state‑level AI regulations and a controversial TikTok sale that underscored geopolitical tensions over data flows. The latter half of 2025 and early 2026 marked a turn toward enforcement, with record EU fines, new African privacy law, and a series of regulatory moves that tightened rules, targeted cross‑border messaging, and demanded AI safety, setting the stage for ongoing battles over AI‑generated leaks and consumer‑tech surveillance.
