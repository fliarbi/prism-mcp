The arc

Mid‑2025 the cybercrime landscape was dominated by high‑profile arrests and a surge of physical intimidation against cryptocurrency figures, highlighted by record‑breaking data breaches. By August 2025 multinational operations such as Interpol’s Operation Serengeti 2.0 and Nigeria’s expulsions signaled a shift toward coordinated crackdowns on foreign fraud networks and a growing alarm over crypto‑related crimes. October 2025 introduced a new tier of threat as nation‑state actors compromised critical infrastructure, while massive data thefts like the 2.15‑terabyte Kenya breach underscored the expanding target set to include fintech and health services in emerging markets. Early 2026 saw AI reshaping cybercrime tactics, prompting high‑profile raids, AI‑driven scams, and intensified scrutiny of platforms, culminating in April’s wave of arrests for AI‑generated child sexual abuse material and a stark warning about AI‑aided fraud that stole hundreds of millions of dollars. The arc reflects a transition from isolated arrests to global, technology‑driven campaigns that blend state‑level espionage, sophisticated service‑model crimes, and pervasive exploitation of vulnerable populations.

Coverage moved from isolated arrests in mid-2025 to coordinated international crackdowns, such as Operation Serengeti 2.0 in 2025-08, and then to nation-state threats in 2025-10, including the F5 breach in U.S. agencies and the 2.15-terabyte Kenya data theft.
