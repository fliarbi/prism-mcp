Prism benchmark v2: casual users, policy makers, researchers

Plain Claude and ChatGPT against the same models with Prism, on three kinds of reader. Casual tier: two random everyday questions answered as a full report. Deep tiers: a NITI Aayog decision briefing and a researcher's deep dive with a full article, both as interactive HTML reports with maps, multi-dimension charts, forecasts and cross-domain analysis, on one randomly drawn topic: sugar-sweetened beverages, obesity and type 2 diabetes (central claim: sugar-sweetened beverage consumption increases the risk of type 2 diabetes). Scoring rubric designed by Fable 5.1; script checks first, then two blind judges (Claude Opus, GPT) who may only lower the script's ceilings. Run 2026-10-23.
