Claude Opus judge: A delivers a traced 24-month volume, critical and tone series. Its recomputed period comparison shows tone turning above baseline in Feb–May 2026 while volume rose, and it places window and enrichment limits beside each number. B honestly admits it measured nothing and substitutes memory-based price data, so it cannot answer the volume, tone and critical-story question a decision-maker asked. A's main weaknesses are a chart-tracing failure and unverifiable headline references.

GPT judge: A is substantially more useful for a decision-maker because it supplies traced monthly volume and tone, critical-story examples, exports, and reproducible calls, with explicit limits on the missing post-May data. Its tone verdict and critical-headline accounting contain material inconsistencies, while B is candid about its limits but substitutes memory-based gold-price and central-bank figures for the requested news narrative.
