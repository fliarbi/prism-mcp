Claude Opus judge: A ran the database queries across all four corpora, so its trends, maps, forecast and article Results trace and are connected into a quantified studied-versus-reported argument. B was transparently honest but measured nothing: its numbers are recalled and its charts describe only its own reference list. For a researcher whose reviewers will check every number, A is the only usable deliverable, despite its untraced export cells and chart.

GPT judge: B is the stronger decision aid because it reports measured research and news trends, geography, evidence layers, and executable follow-ups, while A is transparent but largely a memory-based literature map. B still needs correction: its chart/export checks fail, and several headline comparisons and evidence tallies overreach or blur the underlying query scopes.
